## §6. Lifecycle

**State:** GROWING (entered at 1.3). A *season* is three months; the states and the retirement rule count in seasons. The states, each with its entry line — a state with no written criterion cannot be entered honestly:
- BORN — the catalogue exists and is checked; no recorded look has yet been made on anything but the catalogue.
- GROWING — at least one recorded look on an artefact that is not this catalogue, in which a *seen* finding changed that artefact's next version, both the look and the change on record.
- MATURE — cited by more than one consumer, with a season of recorded looks and no change to any lens's question.
- DECLINING — a season with no look recorded.
- DEAD — superseded (*Successor*, below) or retired as a whole.
The state line in this file's front matter is read by the witness (claim 16), not merely asserted.

**Admission.** A candidate becomes a lens only if (a) its question is not already asked by an existing lens, and (b) its section reads identically when the artefact under review is a trust hierarchy, a verification suite, a chapter draft and a hiring plan. Failing (a) it becomes a prompt inside the existing lens, with its lineage noted. Failing (b) it is advice, not a lens. It must also name its exits — when the looking is done — or it is a question, not a lens. Test (a) is made pairwise and in writing, the candidate's first question set against each existing lens's first question; it is never assumed from a summary, and the burden lies on folding, not on keeping.

**Merge.** When two lenses are found to ask one question, the later one folds into the earlier; its lineage is added to the survivor's line and §7 records the fold. Tags are never reused.

**Read before deciding.** A fold, a drop, a merge or a retirement is decided only after its source has been read in full. A one-line summary, a remembered chapter, or a state's name alone is never grounds; a verdict formed before the reading is an agenda, and `RR` sends it out of the room.

**Retire.** A lens with no citation in four seasons is retired to §7 with its reason. Its tag is never reused.

**Successor.** If this catalogue is superseded, the successor is named in the front matter, every tag is carried or explicitly retired there, and this file stops changing.

**Existence is derived; only the decision is typed.** What the source — [AI_WAY_OF_BUILDING_V1](https://github.com/enigmatic-saini/AI_WAY_OF_BUILDING_V1) — contains is read by a script into the manifest. A person decides each entry's fate and writes that decision into the ledger. Nothing may be marked kept, folded or not carried unless the manifest names it, and nothing in the manifest may be left without a fate.

**Scope honesty.** This file does not say what to think. A section that drifts into telling the reader what the right answer is loses its place at the next check.

"One tag per lens; two lenses that ask one question are one lens" removed from §0.
