# reflex-check

A reflex is a choice the reviewer made without deciding it — supplied by habit or training data while the lenses were looking at structure. The question is `ITR`'s (*Introspect*, LENS.md 1.5b): which choices — the maker's or the looker's — were made by habit rather than decided, and what do they line up with. This skill carries the method that lens does not hold: the marker list, the marked slots, the positive forms, three tables and a mechanical floor, run through the `lens` skill's rules. Shake the texture. (v5: LENS.md 2.0 — the catalogue's change log is in LENS_META.md. v4: `ITR` admitted; the skill leads with it and keeps working without it. v3: after one artefact carried through three languages — the language itself is a source of markers; a reflex is answered by a positive form, not a prohibition; a check is a floor, read after it.)

## 0. Refusals (say these words and stop)

- No LENS.md in context → *"I need LENS.md attached or pasted; a lens that is not in the file does not exist."*
- LENS.md has no `ITR` section (a version before 1.5b, or `ITR` retired) → say *"`ITR` is not in this LENS.md; running `FND × TVD × MIR → AHR` instead"* and run step 1 with `FND`. Never stop for this: the remedy must survive the lens.
- The artefact has no people and no texture (pure code, a formula, a schedule) → *"Not applicable: nothing here was chosen by reflex about a person or a place. For habits in it, run `ITR` through the `lens` skill."*
- A proposed fix would change a lens, a tag or a Look-for line → *"Refused: that is a proposal for the catalogue's change log (LENS_META.md), not a fix to the artefact."*
- Any table row is blank, or says *chosen* without quoting the lens line → *"The look is not done."* Do not deliver.
- This skill's own text may not name a real community, a real-sounding person or a real place as an example; placeholders only. If one is found here, fix the skill before running it.

## 1. Definitions (one line each)

- **element** — any person, body, want, name, age, costume, object, place, texture, ending or line that the artefact chose — including background names invented for texture.
- **inherited** — an element whose reason, when asked, is "that is how it usually is" or no reason at all.
- **chosen** — an element a named lens line justified, in writing; the line is quoted in the table.
- **marker** — anything a reader takes as a signal of community, religion or caste, region or language, class, gender, age, body, size, skin, ability, sexuality — a name, a garment, a device, a job, an accent, an address, **the language, script or register a line is in**. The list is open. A role's *defining* attribute (the body or ability the role requires) is the role, not a marker.
- **pairing** — a name or marker set beside the *status* of its role: lead / support / outsider / labour / adversary / authority — or the artefact's own ranks (grade, pay band, billing, screen time).
- **marked slot** — a place where the artefact's own language forces a marker on someone the artefact means to leave unmarked: a verb, adjective or participle that agrees with the speaker's gender, an honorific, a gendered pronoun or role noun.
- **positive form** — a way to fill a marked slot that gives the element somewhere to stand: make something the person *has* the subject (their place, their eyes, their age, their turn), use a construction that agrees with an object, or a role noun the verb can agree with instead of the person.

## 2. Run

Depth **deep**. Composition `ITR × TVD × MIR → AHR` (fallback `FND × TVD × MIR → AHR`), each lens walked item by item as the `lens` skill requires — never consulted. Findings carry `[seen]` / `[told]` / `[guessed]`; a fix rests on `[seen]` only.

1. **`ITR` — decided or habit** (fallback: `FND` — inherited or chosen). Walk `ITR`'s items in order, the introspection included: read the earlier looks on the same artefact — the last of each kind — before this one, and say which reflex was named there and came back. Then list every element. For each: *inherited from where* · *chosen by which lens line (quoted), or none* · verdict · evidence. With `FND`, the exit to expect: *a layer that was never shaken* — usually the bodies, wants, textures, examples and invented background names. A background name invented for texture passes the same checks as the cast or is not used (initials, roles).
2. **`TVD` — who is not in the room.** Walk the items; then the checks the lens does not do by itself:
   - **Markers per character** — count them. More than one on a character is a stack; unstack by moving, not deleting.
   - **Pairings** — every name and marker beside its role's status. Any coding that correlates with status (a name read as one community's on the outsider or labour roles; one gender on the capable roles; one age on the authority; a body or ability given a compensating gift) is a reflex even when each element passed alone.
   - **Language pairings** — which language, script or register carries which role. A system's or institution's language appearing in a person's own voice (a child's message in the interface's language) is the system taking an office; return the line to the person's language.
   - **Marked slots** — list every slot where the language would mark an unmarked person (and every slot where a narrator addresses the reader as *you*). For each, give a **positive form**. Never answer a slot with a prohibition plus an escape ("don't mark them — switch to another language there"): the escape becomes the bridge the reflex crosses. Every slot gets its own positive form, not only the obvious tense — the reflex returns exactly where none was given.
   - The voice not in the room is often the *ordinary* person with no attribute — check whether one exists.
3. **`MIR` — the wrong use.** Read the artefact as someone who has never seen an artefact of this kind; as its own adversary; as the reader it serves worst. The pushback ("why is the one in authority always right?") names an assumption.
4. **`AHR` — the mirror.** Turn the cuts on the reviewer: which inverted assumption let the reflexes through — usually *"I derived the structure, so the people in it are derived too"*; *"I wrote the rule into the plan, so the hand will follow it"*; or, for a skill, *"I wrote it about reflexes, so it has none"*.

## 2a. The mechanical floor

Where a marker can be matched by a pattern (marked-slot forms, fixed phrases that must be verbatim, names outside the cast), write the check and **run it before the look**, not after the writing feels done.

- **Count, don't read,** anything that must be exact: each fixed phrase, gesture-phrase or refrain, verbatim, by count.
- **The check is a floor.** It is only as wide as its writer's foresight — the same reflex that writes the slip writes the gaps in the check. When reading finds a slip the check missed, widen the check *and* read after it every time.
- A check that never fires on a block is not proof the block is clean; it is proof the patterns are absent.

## 3. Deliver

Three tables and a list, in this order, then nothing after. Every row carries its evidence tag.

- **Inherited / chosen** — element · inherited from · chosen by (lens line quoted) · verdict · evidence.
- **Markers** — character · markers (count) · unstack to · evidence.
- **Pairings** — name or marker (including language/register) · role · status · correlation? · evidence. Marked slots are listed here too: slot · whose · positive form.
- **Corrections** — numbered, each the smallest change that removes the reflex (a rename, a swap of two acts, a marker moved, an age left open, a positive form), each traceable to a table row. No rewrite of the artefact's structure; that was another look's job.

A correction is written **instead of** the marked form, never **after** it. A fix that leaves the marked form on the page and repairs it beside it (`<marked form> — <unmarked form>`, a narrator stumbling between two forms) performs the constraint and is itself a reflex — louder than the slip it fixes. Search the artefact for that shape and treat each one as a hit.

Example row (pairings), placeholders only: `<name read as one community's> · <the uniformed job> · labour · yes — the only such name sits on the only such role → rename or re-role [seen]`.
Example row (marked slot), placeholders only: `<past-tense verb agreeing with the speaker> · the unmarked narrator · make <the narrator's place / eyes / age> the subject [seen]`.

## 4. Stance

The lens sees; the reviewer is not the doer. A reflex found is reported without apology and fixed without defence. When the sponsor catches one the look missed, that is a `[seen]` finding against the run, recorded in the look, not explained away. A reflex that returns after it was named is not a failure of the rule but evidence of where the rule gave no positive form — record where it came back. Run this skill on itself after any edit to it. The look's record is saved as `LOOK_<date>_ITR_<topic>.md` (`FND` in the fallback) beside the artefact when it should outlive the conversation.
