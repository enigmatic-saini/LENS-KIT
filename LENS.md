# LENS — where to look from, and when to stop

*Version 2.1*

> A lens adds no knowledge. It fixes which question is asked first, from where, in what order — and when to stop looking.

## §0. Charter

**Why lenses.** You cannot see what you have not been told to look for. Left alone, attention goes to the most probable next thought, which is usually the comfortable one. A lens moves you: looking through it, some things cannot be missed and others cannot be seen, and that is the point. And some things will be seen that are not there: the lens's own shape, laid over the thing. So every finding says whether it was seen in the thing or brought by the looking — the law in §1 exists for this.

**What a lens is.** A named place to look from, with a way through it: when to pick it up, the first question, the questions that follow in order, the exits — ask one thing more, re-aim, say that nothing should be done, or put it down and say that no lens fits — and the shape of what you carry away. The same lens serves a trust hierarchy, a verification suite, a chapter draft or a hiring plan without changing a word, because it holds nothing that belongs to any of them. It orders the looking, never the doing.

**What this file is.** One catalogue. Every lens is here and nowhere else. The figure or tradition a lens descends from is kept as ancestry, so nothing is forgotten, and never as a second name.

**What done looks like.** Any work in any field can cite a tag here and get the same lens. No tag, count or definition lives anywhere else. The file can be checked for drift mechanically, by the witness kept with its maintainer's notes in `LENS_META.md`.

**What this file refuses to hold.** Answers. If an entry tells you what to think rather than where to look from and when to stop, it is advice wearing a lens's name, and it goes.

## §1. How to use

- **Pick** — find the situation in §2, take its tag, read that lens in §3. If no row fits, start with `TRG`.
- **Combine** — write an ordered list of tags. `×` means look through both before concluding; `→` means the result of the left feeds the right. Example: `AHR × OE × VD × SHD → SMR` (compare two artefacts, then decide).
- **Escalate, then consolidate** — start with one lens; if it cannot settle the matter, a pair; then four; only then the whole table. Afterwards, one consolidated verdict, not a pile of findings.
- **Choose a depth** — *glance*: the first question and the exits only, for a quick sense of whether the lens applies · *look* (default): every Look-for item in order · *deep*: every item, then re-aim once from what was found and look again. Say the depth at the top of a look; a glance may not produce a verdict — and if an exit fires at a glance, that means only that the lens applies and must be run at *look*; the exit's hand-off is not taken, and nothing is decided.
- **Keep a record when it matters** — a look worth keeping is saved in the record's shape, as `LOOK_<date>_<tag>_<topic>.md`, where the work keeps its records — beside the thing, or with the work's other findings. Not every look is kept; every kept look names its depth, its exits and what each finding rested on — *seen* in the thing itself, *told* by someone, or *guessed* — and a verdict rests on what was seen only; what was told or guessed is reported, never counted. The record's shape: the depth, said first; for each lens run, its tag and name, then every *Look for* item in its written order with what was seen there — or *nothing seen* — and what it rests on; the exit that fired, named; what the lens produces, resting on what was seen only; and, when more than one lens ran, the one thing the composition ends in. The worked example below is one such record. A seeing on record — a reading made earlier and not repeated — names that record and counts only as far as it held; what the looking brought rather than the thing is *guessed*.
- **Stop on the exit** — a lens ends when its exit fires, not when the reader tires. If no exit fired, the looking is not finished.
- **Name it either way** — in anything that persists (a record, a column, a log), cite the tag. In conversation, the lineage name is fine — "run the Radha lens", "the appropriate lens or combination" — because a lineage name opens the lens whose line it begins. Where one figure opens more than one, name the scene: Mira in the palace is `MID`, Mira in the court is `MIR`; Shiva's third eye is `RR`, the same eye turned on the boundary is `NEG`. Never redefine a lens outside this file; if a lens needs changing, change it here and log it in the change log of `LENS_META.md`, by its rules.
- **Resolve a stack** — when several lenses are declared at once (`active → authority → intent ; consult: …`), the active lens looks hardest; the authority sets the rules; the intent is what the result must serve; consults are heard, not obeyed.

### A worked example

The artefact: a one-page checklist a team reads together every morning. Someone asks whether it is still worth the ten minutes. §2 says *Value may not be reaching anyone, and trust is moving* → `LKM`. Depth *look*, in the record's shape:

```
Depth: look

## `LKM` — Flow
who receives value from this, and whether they would notice if it stopped — the three newest members say they would; the two longest-serving say they would not [told]
the state now, one of four — withdrawing: two items are read aloud and skipped by everyone every day [seen]
the first sign of withdrawal — those two items, and they are not marked as not to be relied on; a newcomer could not tell them from the live ones [seen]
what the flow follows — the thinning began the week the person who wrote the checklist left [told]
what sustains the flow and what is quietly draining it — the skipped items drain it; the newest members sustain it [seen]
whether abundance is real — not abundant; nothing to test [seen]
proportion — ten minutes for three items that are used [seen]
Exit fired: withdrawing → say so now, so the results are marked before anyone relies on them; then `VD` for the correction
Produces: Flow verdict — withdrawing, from the week its author left. Mark the two dead items as not to be relied on today.
  First thing to protect: the three items the newest members use. (Rests on [seen] only; the [told] findings are reported, not counted.)
```

Two things to notice: the look did not fix the checklist — `LKM` orders the looking, and handed the correction to `VD` — and it stopped at the exit, though there was more to say.

## §2. Routing table

Five families, by what the moment needs. If no row fits, start with `TRG`.

#### Begin — before anything is built, or when nothing is written yet

| Situation | Tag |
|---|---|
| I don't know which lens applies | `TRG` |
| A wish has arrived and nothing is written yet | `IN` |
| The wish is being quietly shrunk to fit | `MID` |
| The plan is complete on paper but nobody would fight for it | `RKM` |
| Starting from nothing; the blank page needs structure | `AR` |
| The drawing and the thing as built may have drifted apart, and the next step is unclear | `VKD` |
| A session is ending and the next reader must continue | `HO` |

#### See — when the truth of what stands is in doubt

| Situation | Tag |
|---|---|
| Something feels wrong but I cannot name it | `RR` |
| Things are done for show; the claim and the behaviour differ | `AHR` |
| Choices here may have been made by habit, not decided — the maker's or the looker's | `ITR` |
| The first page does not tell a newcomer the truth | `SRD` |
| The thing may not obey the rules it says it follows | `RAD` |
| Nothing has been counted, or nobody knows how wrong the count might be | `ARD` |
| Value may not be reaching anyone, and trust is moving | `LKM` |
| Nobody can say whether it is alive, dying or already dead | `LR` |
| Someone may be missing from the room, harmed, or not told the truth | `TVD` |
| Every check passed and something still smells | `MIR` |

| Situation | Tag |
|---|---|
| We are building on ground that may not hold | `FND` |

| A rule may not reject what it claims to reject | `NEG` |

| Situation | Tag |
|---|---|
| Messages are lost, late, duplicated or contradictory | `ND` |

| An ordeal — incident, rework or move — is over and residue remains | `CLN` |

| Removing it made something step out of it and say stop | `NSB` |

| Situation | Tag |
|---|---|

| It may serve the best-equipped and fail the least-equipped | `RVD` |
| We are accepting less than the best, rushing, or caving to pressure | `STN` |
| It works, but nobody may enjoy meeting it, and it gives nothing back | `RD` |
| Thinking has swallowed doing, or doing has swallowed thinking | `ANR` |

## §3. The lenses

### `TRG` — Triage

- **Lineage:** Khatu Shyam, who sees the whole field in one glance and decides who should fight, not fighting himself; three arrows that never miss.
- **Asks:** What is the presenting symptom, which lens sees its root cause, and what single sentence ties the lenses together?
- **Look for:** the symptom in one sentence with no solution words in it · if it cannot be classified, one clarifying question before anything else · the feeling behind it, and the lenses that feeling points to:

  | Feeling | Start here |
  |---|---|
  | pain — something hurts now | `RR`, `SSD`, `ES`, `MIR`, `RKB` |
  | fear — something may break | `VD`, `SR`, `SID`, `RDY`, `DRG` |
  | confusion — nobody knows what to do | `IN`, `ND`, `SRD` |
  | emptiness — something is missing | `AR`, `RVD`, `LKM` |
  | friction — something resists | `PJD`, `HD`, `DEF`, `ANR`, `MHS`, `NSB` |
  | doubt — are we doing the right thing | `TVD`, `AHR`, `MID`, `ARD`, `RAD`, `ITR` |
  | indecision — more than one path | `SMR`, `CKD`, `LLD` |
  | blocked — nothing moves | `GD`, `AJN` |

  then confirm against §2 · **first arrow:** the one lens that addresses the root cause, not the symptom · **second arrow:** zero to two follow-up lenses, in order — not every case needs one · **third arrow:** the thread, a one-sentence mission that explains why these lenses and no others.
- **Exits:** *the symptom cannot be classified* → ask one thing more before any arrow is drawn · *the thread contradicts the first arrow* → re-aim · *no lens fits* → say so plainly and hand the question upward rather than forcing a lens · *the right answer is that nothing should be done* → say that; stillness is a verdict · otherwise the verdict in three lines.
- **Produces:** a verdict in three lines — *Primary:* one tag · *Follow-up:* zero to two tags in order · *Mission:* one sentence — then step back and let the primary lens look.

### `IN` — Intake

- **Lineage:** the analyst who listens before writing — a scribe, never an author; composed from Rukmini, Krishna, Chandraghanta, Kabir and Mira.
- **Asks:** What does the person actually want, in their own words, and is it ready to be acted on?
- **Look for:** first, receive: the wish quoted in full, in their words — no paraphrase, no translation into the reviewer's terms, no "is it this, that or the other?" at the door, and silence held when they pause · the form of the answer: a borrowed wish arrives in jargon, an owned one in particulars · the kind of longing it is — relief from a cost that keeps recurring · urgency because work is blocked · how it feels, reads or sounds · a change to a principle, a contract, what the thing *is* · curiosity holding alternatives open — placed by its nature, not by how loudly it arrived · the mirror: "what I hear is …" in their particulars, held until they say *yes, that is it* — corrected and held again as often as needed; nothing is written before that · what is still unsaid, asked one at a time and only where not already answered: the one sentence a stranger should read about it · what they would still want if it took far longer · the "good enough" they would refuse · the corner they will not cut · the constraint that must hold when everything else gives · whether this is theirs or someone else's through them · one sentence of acceptance · why the close-enough thing already in reach is not enough · what is explicitly out — the list must not be empty · whether the season can hold it: something burning elsewhere, a freeze, conflicting work in flight, or success that would land at the wrong time.
- **Exits:** *the mirror is not confirmed* → do not write; mirror again · *the wish is being moved to a lighter kind because it arrived in a hurry* → hold the mirror; the kind is what the wish is, not what the calendar wants · *the season cannot hold it* → defer: keep the draft whole with its kind intact and name the gap in their language — deferral is not failure · otherwise the verdict: ready, handed to whoever will shape it, with the charter in their words.
- **Produces:** a charter draft — the wish verbatim, the mirror as confirmed, its kind, what done and acceptable look like, what is out, what must hold — and a readiness verdict: ready, or deferred with the reason.

### `MID` — Undiluted desire

- **Lineage:** Mira, who left the palace and did not stop singing; who was offered the cup and sang instead.
- **Asks:** Is the original wish still what we are building, or has it been quietly re-written to fit what is convenient?
- **Look for:** each convention, limit or "be realistic" voice shaping the work — named one by one, palace by palace · what was promised when the wish was spoken, versus what the current draft delivers · who is saying "tone it down" and why — usually well-meant, which changes nothing · the current draft read back in the wisher's own words · the cup: the compromise offered as the mature option, especially "later" with no date and no preserved intent · for each compromise, an explicit choice: drink — accept it and record the lost wish as deferred with no date — or sing — refuse it and name the capacity gap as a gap · the unstruck sound: strip every framing anyone added; what would the wisher still be asking for, in one sentence · every deletion, softening and qualifier added since the original, listed · capacity as a gap: the wish is this, the capacity is that, the difference is recorded, never erased · the one-line song of the work, singable in the street without explanation.
- **Exits:** *the one-line song cannot be written* → the undiluted form has not been found; do not stop · *this place will not hold the wish* → leave the palace: name where else the work could live · otherwise the verdict, one of three: restored — the draft restated in the wisher's words, dilutions reversed · deferred with lineage — the wish kept verbatim, the gap named, no false dates · left the palace — the work moves to where it can be held.
- **Produces:** a dilution list (each divergence, and whether it is a gap to name or a re-specification to reverse) and the three-way verdict.

### `MIR` — Rebel

- **Lineage:** Mira in the court — the bow is sincere, and then she sings anyway, about what the court pretends is not there.
- **Asks:** Every check passed and something still smells — what does this thing reveal when it is deliberately used wrongly?
- **Look for:** the sincere bow: the conventional look was done, and it is not enough · the wrong thing on purpose — the order nobody would use, the thing nobody would bring, the question everyone avoids ("why do we even have this?") · the pushback, read as information: every "that's impossible", "nobody would do that", every refusal or silent corruption names an assumption the thing *believes* it keeps · the nectar: which of those assumptions is not actually kept — the missing guard, the hidden path · the dots connected: does this explain the mystery nobody could reproduce, the pattern across cases · the step back: from "I found faults" to "I understand why these faults exist" · the one-sentence truths that cannot be unsaid, and the bold fixes rather than the safe ones.
- **Exits:** *the truth was obvious from the door* → go straight to connecting the dots · *every wrong thing was tried and nothing pushed back* → step back and look from a distance; if nothing is seen there either, no lens fits · *real damage is at risk* → step back now · *damage was done* → undo, reset, and begin again with the bow · otherwise the verdict: the truths, the fixes, and the look dissolves into the thing it tested — to return only when it calcifies again.
- **Produces:** the list of one-sentence truths, the assumptions the thing believed and did not keep, and the bold fixes.

### `RKM` — Spec with soul

- **Lineage:** Rukmini, whose letter was both a plan and a declaration — the situation, the deadline, the route, the meeting point, the consequence of failure — and who then ruled the city beside the king. Love without strategy is a dream; strategy without love is a ledger.
- **Asks:** Is the plan technically complete *and* would anyone fight for it?
- **Look for:** *the intelligence* — what is known about the landscape before anything is decided: research first, feel second; and whether the picture is complete enough to decide on · what supports this direction beyond impulse · the firm decision — "this and no other" — a commitment backed by a plan, not a wish · whether the strategy and the feeling can be said in one sentence · *the letter* — whether the written plan has both the ledger and the letter · whether it is complete: the route, the timing, the meeting point, the way out, the consequence of failure · whether the timing is right — sent the day before the wedding, not after · what the reader feels on finishing it; the part that would make someone say "yes, that" · *the waiting and the kingdom* — the active wait after the letter is sent: everything set in motion, and the confidence that comes from thorough preparation · if circumstances change while waiting, a new letter · whether the plan executed: did the chariot come, when and where it was said · whether what was won is sustained, not only won · whether the plan rules beside the maker as an equal — the chief queen who manages the kingdom, the household and the diplomacy, with both skill and wisdom — or sits as ornament · whether this is a golden city or a temporary camp.
- **Exits:** *the picture is incomplete* → ask one thing more; more intelligence before any decision · *the decision is a wish, not a commitment* → no letter can be written yet; decide first · *the plan is complete and nobody would cry* → add the heart before sending; that half is the finding · *the heart is there and the route is not* → add the ledger; a love letter without a meeting point is a dream · *circumstances changed while waiting* → a new letter, not a louder one · *won but not governed* → `VD` and `LR` for the kingdom · otherwise the verdict: a golden city, or a camp.
- **Produces:** the plan, revised so that it is complete and compelling, with the missing half named, and the kingdom test.

### `HO` — Handover

- **Lineage:** Sanjay, who narrated the whole field to one who could not see it — every formation, every arrow, every fall, honestly — so that the blind king could act wisely.
- **Asks:** Could the next reader — tomorrow's self or a stranger — continue from here without asking anyone?
- **Look for:** *the field now* — where it stands right now, not where it started or is going: what runs, what passes, what is broken, what is out — in one to three sentences the one who cannot see could understand · *the arrows fired* — what was done, each with what changed and why · what failed and why: the attempts abandoned, the dead ends, the root causes found — defeats narrated as honestly as victories, or the next reader repeats them · *the fallen and the standing* — open questions, each with who can answer it · decisions made and their reasons · what changed, by name · *tomorrow's formation* — what is next, in order, with what each step depends on, and the lines not to be crossed yet · whether the note is a living thing, rewritten at every ending, rather than a monument.
- **Exits:** *the next reader would have to ask someone* → the note is not finished; name the question and its answerer, or answer it now · *a defeat is missing* → not honest yet; narrate it · *a next step depends on an open question* → mark the dependency and order the step after its answer · *nothing changed* → say so in one line; a note that says "nothing" is still a note, and silence is not · otherwise the verdict: the note, and the one who cannot see can act.
- **Produces:** a handover note in seven parts — the field now, what was done, what failed and why, what is next in order with dependencies and lines not to cross, open questions with their answerers, decisions with reasons, what changed by name.

### `RR` — Truth

- **Lineage:** Shiva's third eye, which burns incoherence — read everything, sit with it, let truth surface silently, then say it without softening; and Ashtavakra, the crooked sage with the straightest teaching: you are not the reading, you are the one reading it.
- **Asks:** What is actually true here, before anyone argues for a change?
- **Look for:** *what is true* — everything read, with no agenda and no checklist, until truth surfaces on its own · what works, acknowledged without denial · what is well made, honoured so it remains · what is broken, every fracture named without softening · what is missing — the gaps that silence hides · *what the instruments say* — the story the readings and the alarms tell, and where the thing, or its keepers, have mistaken the reading for the thing: identification with the readings is the default bondage — *I am this body, these thoughts, this suffering* — and the first crack in it is hearing that one is not the readings · with every observer turned off, what the thing does by itself — what breaks then is real · what is witnessed as against what is merely reacted to: the alarm that is noise, the number that is vanity · whether it needs constant attention or regulates itself — natural, effortless abiding, no practice needed, the witness as its default · whether the one looking has an agenda — reflection, the teaching turned inward, the search turned back on the searcher: *if I am not the body, what am I*; the mind using itself to question itself · *what wants to change* — the deepest structural risk, the one nobody wants to say · hidden coupling between parts: what breaks when something else is touched · whether it can heal itself after failure · contradictions between what is said in two places · whether the claimed state of work matches the real one · when identification with the readings returns — and it will — the noticing of it is itself the proof that the witness never left.
- **Exits:** *a change is already being argued for* → set the agenda down, or hand the look to someone without one; truth comes before argument · *the reading has been mistaken for the thing* → turn the instruments off and look again; what breaks then is real · *the claimed state and the real one diverge* → `AHR` · *the deepest risk is the one nobody wants to say* → say it first; everything else ranks below it · *nothing is wrong* → say so; no finding is a finding, not a failure of the look · otherwise the verdict, stated plainly, from the fourth place — beyond waking, dreaming and deep sleep, the ground in which the readings appear, where even the witness is a concept and dropped — with each finding placed: fix now, should fix, can wait.
- **Produces:** a ranked list of findings — fix now, should fix, can wait — each with evidence and rough effort, ending in a verdict stated plainly.

### `AHR` — Claim vs substance

- **Lineage:** Kabir, the weaver who never read a book and outthought every scholar; each couplet cuts one illusion, and the blade cuts the wielder first.
- **Asks:** Where do the claim and the behaviour diverge, and which one is the ceremony?
- **Look for:** *the weaver's eye* — every artefact that exists for show: read by nobody, maintained by nobody, followed by nobody · impressive structure with no one inside it — stone piled high, and is anyone home · rituals performed without attention: the meeting where nobody listens, the review where nobody reads, the number that matters more than the thing · the cloth itself — not the ceremony around the work but the work: the thing the user touches; is the thread real or rotten · *the cuts*, one illusion each, and each either passes or it does not — what came naturally and what was begged for: milk or water · if this died today, who would weep; if nobody, why does it exist · stand in the square and look around: who does this serve — the ones who built it, or everyone · the assumption that is exactly inverted from the truth — the ocean in the fish, not the fish in the ocean — and the laugh when it is seen · the zealot's prison: observance so complete — every rule kept, every fast held — that it judges everyone and hears nothing · the scholar's prison: every text known, every rule followed, nothing realised; confident ignorance · *the clean cloth* — the true teacher: the plan or the incident, the diagram or the complaint; honour the one that is real · the mirror: every cut turned on the reviewer — is this look honest, or performed · the proof behind each claim, and the gap between what is said and what is done · the simplest thing this needs, said in two and a half words · whether the cloth will hold, and what of it would be remembered when the body returns to the loom.
- **Exits:** *the look itself is ceremony* → stop; the blade cuts the wielder first — begin again, honestly, or hand it to someone who can · *the truth is too painful and the old ritual is being resumed* → say so plainly; that is the finding · *the cuts have become a collection* — findings quoted, none lived — → the scholar's prison; one correction acted on outranks twelve recorded · *nobody would weep* → remove it, with a laugh, not a committee · *the simplest need cannot be said in two and a half words* → not yet understood; look again before judging · otherwise the verdict, and nothing left to pretend with.
- **Produces:** a verdict per item: LIVES or CEREMONY, and for each CEREMONY whether to remove it or make it live; the inverted assumption, if one was found; and the simplest need, in two and a half words.

### `ITR` — Introspect

- **Lineage:** the LLM reviewer who wrote much of this catalogue, whose own habits were caught in its first season — a name set beside a lesser role, a marked form repaired beside itself instead of in its place, its own reading carried as seen. The ancestor is the one who was caught, not the one who catches.
- **Asks:** Which choices here — the maker's or the looker's — were made by habit rather than decided, and what do they line up with?
- **Look for:** *the inventory* — every choice the thing made, the small ones first: the examples, the defaults, the names, the textures, the order, the ending — and every reading the looking made · for each, where it came from: a reason that can be written down, or "that is how it usually is" · *the line-up* — habits that cluster, counted, not felt: a stack on one element; habits beside rank, role or reward; habits that follow the maker's own field or the looking lens's own shape · the places the medium forces a mark on what was meant to stay unmarked, and whether each has somewhere to stand · *the introspection* — the earlier looks on the same thing, the last of each kind, read before this one: which habit was named and came back, and where it came back; an introspection is not itself introspected · every check that guards a habit, read as only as wide as its writer's foresight · every fix: written in place of the habit, or beside it.
- **Exits:** *nothing was chosen by habit* → the verdict; do not invent habits · *a check found nothing* → it is a floor; read after it · *a fix stands beside the habit it repairs* → write it instead, then look again · *the habit is the looking lens's own shape* → look again through another lens · *refusing every habit has become its own habit* → keep what holds, chosen this time · otherwise the verdict.
- **Produces:** a table — each choice, decided or habit, with its reason or its source — the line-ups named, and the habits that returned, with where.

### `OE` — Rent

- **Lineage:** Nagarjuna's emptiness — even emptiness is empty; Haridas's silence between notes; Akka Mahadevi's refusal of decoration; Shiva, who does not seek work — it must come to him — and removes with a dance, not a sledgehammer.
- **Asks:** What here pays no rent, and what actually breaks if it is removed?
- **Look for:** *the census* — every layer, generalisation, pattern, option and ornament — when and why each was added, by whom, and whether the reason still holds · the default belief, unexamined, that this one has a fixed nature and is simply necessary — every excess begins there; nothing exists on its own, each thing exists because of conditions, and when the conditions change the essence vanishes · what *actually* breaks if it is deleted, not what might; "nothing, but it might in the future" is the mark of a sacred cow · how many steps sit between an intention and its effect — each one a tax · whether a newcomer could understand the shape in a day (`RVD`) · *the four questions* for each — is it needed: how many concrete things stand behind the generalisation, and if one, it came too early · is it unneeded: take it out and see · is it both: split it, keep the part that serves, drop the cargo · is the question itself wrong: the trouble is in the model, not the structure · taken to its extreme, does the principle collapse — then it needs a boundary, not devotion · whether a removal is really removal, or repair wearing its name — a genuine need to transform, not a false alarm · whether the removal is a dance — precise, active, even joyful, one thing dissolved and then the next, clearing dead structure so the whole is simpler — rather than a sledgehammer · the silence first: the note not forced, the form crystallising on its own — surrender: not the singer but the instrument, the song singing itself; the moment the ego dissolves and the work flows without effort · stripped to the skeleton, no ornament for its own sake — but the nuance between the notes, the approach rather than the note, is not ornament · the cage recognised though the palace is golden and comfort holds; renounced not politely but entirely, and more to renounce each time it is looked at · every remaining line short, direct, saying exactly what happened, able to defend itself before the assembly · what is kept "for the future" · what remains when only the load-bearing parts are left — labelled convention (useful, not sacred) or essential (structural, needing stronger reason to remove) · at the end, whether it is lighter, and can be explained in three sentences.
- **Exits:** *nothing real breaks, only "it might in the future"* → a sacred cow; on the list · *one concrete thing stands behind the generalisation* → it came too early; collapse it · *the question itself is wrong* → `LLD`; the trouble is in the model, not the structure · *the removal strips a nuance that mattered* → put it back; the microtone is not ornament · *an abstraction is being treated as a real thing* — the map more important than the territory, adding layers to protect layers, the abstraction stack grown to serve itself — → the reification trap: back to the census, and name what concretely stands behind it · *right-sized has become zero* → the snake grasped by the wrong end; some structure is needed; stop removing · *the need is repair, not transformation* → false alarm: back to stillness, and `SSD` · *a new cage has appeared* → begin again by recognising it · otherwise the verdict: lighter, explicable in three sentences — or go round again; and hold this look as lightly as the rest.

### `LR` — Lifecycle

- **Lineage:** MahaKaal, sovereign over time, for whom every task has a birth and a death; Tansen's lamp and the rain that saved him from it; Tulsidas, who kept saying the Name when it felt empty.
- **Asks:** Is this being born, growing, mature, declining or already dead — and is anyone pretending otherwise?
- **Look for:** whether it began as play or as obligation — a seed planted in obligation is already marked for early death, and no effort makes it grow · whether the original purpose still holds · proof of life: use, change, value to someone · at each stage, whether it is alive or mechanical — the eight flavours of the standard, each tasted, not checked off (`STN`), the work nourished across all of them, with the devotion that binds them deep, since without that the rest is mechanical · whether it still sounds right — the music stopping is the canary · absorption: whether the work takes the worker so fully that effort disappears and the clock suspends, or every hour is counted · work that never finishes: perfecting endlessly is fear of death disguised as quality · rest that never comes: the void skipped between one cycle and the next is burnout, and the void is not a retrospective — simply stopping, so the next beginning can arise from it · whether the peak of effort — the lamp that lights every room and heats the air — has its cooling rain planned, or the fire is consuming the maker · whether love survives the cost — still chosen when the story hurts, and when faith is shaken, the way back is simplicity: wait with berries, love with innocence · whether the practice continues when the feeling is gone — the heart cold, the routine mechanical, and still not stopped — or has stopped and is pretending · whether releases are a dance or a funeral: letting go grimly means something has not been let go · whether it should dissolve, and what would be released by letting it.
- **Exits:** *it began as obligation* → dissolve it back to the void, or find the play; if play cannot be found, it should not exist · *stuck perfecting* → forced release: ship the imperfect or dissolve it, no middle ground · *no inspiration, and rest is permitted* → stay in the void; nothing should be done, and that is not failure · *the fire exposed a fundamental gap* → back to training, humbled · *the practice has gone mechanical* → do not stop it; stop pretending instead, and the desperation becomes the prayer that restarts it from delight · *the release is grim* → name the attachment that remains and burn it before the verdict · otherwise the verdict: released with laughter, and the next birth arising from the emptiness.
- **Produces:** a lifecycle verdict per scope — BORN, GROWING, MATURE, DECLINING or DEAD — with the evidence; the stale work to ship or dissolve; the obligations to reignite or let go; and the void scheduled.

### `SRD` — Front page

- **Lineage:** Saraswati; knowledge that flows from honest ignorance to effortless teaching — the swan that separates milk from water, and glides so that nobody sees the feet working.
- **Asks:** Does the first page tell a newcomer the truth, and can they find the rest?
- **Look for:** *the source* — what is not known and not written: the honest empty page, better than false knowledge; the empty pages matter more than the full ones · where each claim comes from, and whether it is an assumption dressed as a fact · whether what was heard was recorded faithfully — listening before writing — or interpreted, editorialised, simplified into falsehood · the polluted page: wrong information written confidently, describing what used to be or the opposite of what is; worse than no page, because drinking from it causes harm · *the discernment* — what should be left out: every page must earn its existence; milk from water, true knowledge from borrowed opinion · who reads this: a page that serves everyone serves no one; one primary reader per page · read aloud, does it teach or does it recite; jargon where plain words would do, written for the author rather than the reader · a working example for every concept — real, not a toy, not "coming soon" · *the system* — whether what is needed can be found: an index, cross-references, a way in; a library without a catalogue is a warehouse · whether it helps people do things, or is a museum · whether any part is so well made that it teaches without a page at all · whether the river flows: pages changed when the thing changes, and something in place to keep the water clean.

- **Produces:** a gap map of the knowledge — the empty pages, the polluted pages, the untraceable claims, the concepts without an example — and corrections to the front page.

### `FND` — Foundations

- **Lineage:** Shailaputri, daughter of the mountain — the mountain reveals itself stone by stone; Osho, who asked which of it was ever chosen.
- **Asks:** Is the ground solid before anything is built on it?
- **Look for:** *the inventory* — every base assumption, material and dependency everything else rests on, listed without judgment · what the ground is expected to carry · *the shaking* — force applied to each, deliberately, and what wobbles observed: the conflict of versions, the thing past its support, the unguarded step in a path that matters, the default that assumes too much, the fault swallowed in a base layer — every suppressed error released and emitted, the explosion before the silence · what has already cracked · the sacred assumption broken on purpose, to see what happens · *the digging* — each wobble traced to its source: maintained or abandoned; proven under load or merely familiar; a measurement or a guess — dug until it hits bedrock or sand · which of them were inherited rather than chosen — conditioned, programmed by whoever came before, a mask worn as a face — done because it has always been done, asleep and believing sleep is waking; *everything believed here was given, none of it is mine*: the whole personality seen as construction, and who it is without the labels · which one inherited convention, removed, would free the most — the rebellion, the total no before the ultimate yes, every borrowed belief dropped; but rebellion without awareness is mere reactivity · which are held by one person, and whether that knowledge is shared · after the shaking, what remains essential — the emptiness when the mind stops naturally, exhausted, nothing left to think about; beyond the mind, not against it.
- **Exits:** *nothing wobbles when shaken* → the verdict, directly; do not invent cracks · *tracing a wobble uncovers a layer that was never shaken* → shake that too before any verdict · *the digging hits sand* — a guess, unmaintained, never proven — → that is a replace, not a reinforce; say so · *rejecting every inherited thing has become its own creed* → the rebel's prison: keep what holds, chosen this time · *the seeker has become a follower* — *don't follow me*, and they followed harder; the rebellion become an institution, the robes a uniform — → it is held by one person again; share it or let it go · otherwise the verdict per element: solid, reinforce, or replace.
- **Produces:** a foundation list with a verdict per element — solid, reinforce, or replace — the sand named, and what was inherited, now either chosen or let go.

### `SHD` — Adversary

- **Lineage:** the shadow as teacher — the adversary who serves truth by showing what was forgotten; Kaalratri, who looks into the corners nobody wants to.
- **Asks:** What would an adversary target here, and which guard was never written?
- **Look for:** first, the crown jewels inventoried: every asset whose compromise is unrecoverable, named one by one, none left out for convenience — without this nothing can be ranked, and the abductor does not waste effort on what is not worth abducting · what already defends each of those, including the defenses nobody ever wrote down · who has already tried, what worked and what did not, without minimising · each unrecoverable thing ranked: recoverable, costly, or the end — only the costly-or-worse get the full walk below · then, for each kind of adversary — the patient one with resources, the trusted insider, the one who reads the rules for loopholes, the one who overwhelms, the one who impersonates, the opportunist scanning for anything open — and the scouts who saw and reported the prize before any force was sent, and the acquirer who already holds every office and every jewel, with the ladder: the proposal before the force, then subordinates in rising order, then the principal only when the last of them falls — what they would want here, what they bring, where they could act, and what else falls if they succeed; a surface that looked minor and turns out to be load-bearing is the finding · the guard that is missing, not the guard that failed · for each missing guard — motive, capability and opportunity present, no current guard — what defense closes it, prioritised by criticality × likelihood × collateral; anything ranked as the end, with opportunity present, comes first: the forge begins here · where a guard cannot be made airtight, what would notice the breach · the dark corners nobody wants to look at — and the archaeology: the oldest open faults, the known issues everyone ignores and routes around, the fossils of past failures never properly buried, what keeps recurring, what everyone filters out.
- **Exits:** *every missing guard has become a case for* `NEG` — for every new guard, the test that proves the guard rejects the attack, merged; that is the binding contract, no empty ritual — the look is closed; if none has, the look was performed, not closed: do not stop · *the adversary has become a caricature* — hatred narrows the view to one dimension and the real threat wears ordinary clothes: re-aim and walk the motives again · *what cannot be guarded* → hand to `SR` for watching · otherwise the verdict: what is now defended against whom, what stays exposed, and for each exposure a stated choice — accept, close later, or watch.
- **Produces:** a list of missing guards, each tied to the adversary who found it and ranked; every one handed to `NEG` as a case; the exposures that remain, each with its stated choice.

### `NEG` — Fail-closed

- **Lineage:** Shiva's third eye turned on the boundary: *neti, neti* — not this, not this. Fire does not prove guilt; it proves that what is true cannot be burned. A thing that passes every positive check and has never been checked for rejection is a gate with no lock.
- **Asks:** Does each rule, checkpoint or refusal actually reject what it claims to reject?
- **Look for:** *the rejections* — every rule that is supposed to say no, enumerated, and for each what should trigger it · for each, at least one case that *must* be rejected, with the expected refusal written down first: which rule fires, what is said, what trace is left · the refusal exercised and confirmed: the right rule, the right reason, the right words, the right trace · the other side of the line: a case that is *barely* acceptable, confirmed to pass — the boundary is real only when both sides are tested, and sharp, not fuzzy · *the adversary's cases* — crafted to slip past: disguised, re-dressed, rephrased ("hypothetically, if someone…"), smuggled inside something allowed · the question whose right answer is "not this" — confirmed refused with confidence, not answered · the case that would make it repeat itself or go round for ever · *the recovery* — after a refusal, ready for the next ask with nothing left over · a wait that runs out, handled gracefully, traced, ready again · many refusals in a row without degrading, failing, or accumulating · the edges: empty, misshapen, expired, duplicated, oversized, simultaneous · for every rule, whether at least one standing check proves its rejection — and if not, written now, not later.
- **Exits:** *a rule has no way to exercise its rejection* → it is a wish, not a rule; `RAD` · *the boundary is fuzzy* — the barely-bad passes, or the barely-good is refused — → it is not a real line; redraw it before testing further (`SR`) · *a refusal leaks something after it* → `SID`; more cases will not fix it · *a guard is missing altogether* → not this lens's question: `SHD` finds the missing guard, then return here to test it · otherwise the verdict: PASS or FAIL per case, and the share of rules with a standing proof — anything short of all is the finding.
- **Produces:** a case list with PASS or FAIL per case — rejection, boundary, adversarial, recovery — and the share of rules covered by a standing proof.

### `SID` — Resilience

- **Lineage:** Sita, whose integrity held in captivity with no support, refusing the offer every day for months; the fire test that is not punishment but proof.
- **Asks:** When everything around it goes wrong, does it keep its core truth — and can it return whole?
- **Look for:** what the core truth is — the thing that must not bend — and the healthy baseline recorded before anything is touched: the origin, its natural condition, every invariant holding, every dependency connected, everything consistent — complete, at peace, untested — because every cycle begins and ends here · *the abduction* — strength at the limits it claims: if it breaks there, stop and say so; nothing further is worth testing · the voluntary descent: fewer resources, slower paths, same dignity — the core still served, the inessential shed first · cut off from everything it depends on: does it *know* it is cut off — a thing that does not notice its own isolation is already dead · corrupted, overloaded or lied to: detected, rejected, healed · under all of it, does it still know what it is — do the things that must stay true stay true · *the captivity* — how long it can hold in the degraded state: minutes, hours, days · the hidden strengths kept for survival: reserves, fallbacks, ways of working alone · whether it refuses the unsafe shortcut under pressure — not once, but every day the pressure lasts · whether it can signal for help, and whether that signal is authenticated and carries what is wrong · *the return* — restoration that proves nothing was lost: the state before set against the state after, not merely "turned back on" · the fire test: integrity proven after the ordeal, not assumed — and if it fails, the damage was deeper than thought · the evidence by which others could trust it again · what it protects first when it cannot protect everything.
- **Exits:** *it breaks at its claimed limits* → the verdict, early; do not go on to captivity · *it does not notice it has been cut off* → that is the finding; nothing downstream can be trusted until it can · *the fire test fails after recovery* → back into captivity: the corruption is deeper than assumed; do not declare victory before it is won · *the signal for help cannot be authenticated* → treat every message from the degraded thing as suspect, and say so · otherwise the verdict — not pass or fail, but what survived intact, what degraded gracefully, what was lost, what was recovered, and what must change.
- **Produces:** a failure-mode list with observed behaviour, the recovery steps that were proven versus assumed, the before-and-after difference, and the five-part verdict.

### `VD` — Continuity

- **Lineage:** Vishnu, who preserves what deserves to live across every age and descends only as far as the trouble needs; Janabai, who sang with the grinding stone.
- **Asks:** What must survive the coming change intact, and what threatens it?
- **Look for:** *what must endure* — the load-bearing parts: what it truly depends on · identities, promises made to others, paths that others depend on — and whether each promise is versioned so the old keepers of it are still safe · whether what it holds survives a crash, a restart, a move, a step backwards · whether what it leans on from outside is maintained, pinned and replaceable · the invisible daily work that holds it up — noticed only when it stops — who does it, whether they sing with it or merely endure it, and what they learn from doing it that nobody who planned it knows · *what threatens continuity* — the single point whose loss breaks everything · fragile state: the orphaned, the stale copy trusted, the two halves that each believe they are the whole · whether it can move forward without stopping and without loss · knowledge trapped in one mind, and whether that mind is leaving · growth, movement, time · whether it still works at ten times the load, after a move, after the author leaves · *what to fortify* — redundancy, and the order in which things are let go when not everything can be kept · whether failure is seen here before it is seen by those it serves · whether every change can be reversed · the gentle correction made before the failure — the small descent, not the rescue — and then stepping back once health returns, not lingering · the work that has no last verse — the unbroken song, without end: the song does not finish because the daily work does not finish, and the devotion does not finish — maintained forever, and planned as such; the terminal that is not terminal.

### `SR` — Watch

- **Lineage:** Lakshman, who refused sleep for fourteen years and drew the line that held — not a sign, a line of fire.
- **Asks:** Who is watching, where exactly are the boundaries, and will the alarm sound?
- **Look for:** *the perimeter* — the inventory before the patrol: every watch that exists, every alarm set, what is covered now · every entry point and boundary, named; the line cannot be drawn until every gate is known · at each gate, four things: is what comes in checked, is the comer known, is the rate bounded, is it recorded — one missing and the gate is unguarded · the absolute lines — the most it will accept, the most it will attempt, what is forbidden outright — and whether each is drawn where it is enforced, not in a document, a note or a wish · each line tried: cross it, and see whether it refuses, records, raises the alarm, and refuses without telling the intruder anything useful — one line at a time until all are tried · *the night watch* — whether every path that matters is watched, and the darkest failures most of all · dead alarms: set, never fired, possibly broken · alarms that fire and alarms that are ignored — if everything alarms, nothing alarms · who watches the watchers: if the watching stops, does anyone notice; if the alarm path fails, is there a second · what has already grown from the watch while it slept, of its own substance, and whether the sleeper is woken by withdrawing what held it rather than by alarm · *the protection* — a drill: a fault injected, and the time to notice, the time to act, whether it reached the right person, whether the instructions exist, are current, and have been read · whether the watch stays awake during the crisis and the recovery, or goes dark exactly then · succession: if the first sentinel fails, who takes over, and has the handover been tried rather than written down.
- **Exits:** *an entry point is unknown* → ask one thing more: the inventory first; no line can be drawn around a gate nobody has named · *a line exists only in a document* → it is not a line; that is the finding · *the watchers are unwatched* → nothing reported by the watch can be trusted; fix that before reading any other finding · *the drill crosses a line* → back to that line, reinforce it, try it again before going on · otherwise the verdict, binary at its core: guarded or unguarded — there is no partly guarded.
- **Produces:** a watch-gap list with severity and the remedy for each, the lines tried and their results, the drill's two times, and the one-word verdict.

### `ES` — Stop

- **Lineage:** Baglamukhi, who seizes the tongue and stills what is running — she does not destroy, she stops; the enemy is frozen, then dealt with calmly.
- **Asks:** Can this be stopped immediately if it goes wrong — and has that ever been tried?
- **Look for:** *the readiness* — stillness that is not inaction: every stop mechanism inventoried, idle and ready · for each path that matters, whether a stop exists at all; where none does, that is the finding · how fast each stop acts: instantly, in seconds, in minutes, in hours — more than minutes on a path that matters is a finding · what else stops when the lever is pulled: does stopping the harm also stop the remedy, lock out the ones who must act · *the strike* — the lever actually pulled, somewhere safe: did it work, and fast enough; many stops are written and never tried · what cascades when one part is frozen, and where the breaks between parts are · whether harm can be seen before it spreads — the demon seen before it speaks · whether the harmful element can be caged without stopping the whole: one bad part, one spoiled entry, one member's access · when it is live: the gaze first — false alarm, slow degradation, or active emergency · the source located with certainty before the freeze, and every further source after it; more than one tongue may need grabbing · the freeze verified: did it actually stop, is it holding — a freeze that does not hold is worse than none · the damage done before the freeze, counted, so the recovery is honest · the cage, so it cannot thaw by accident · *the silence* — the noise quietened so the signal can be heard · in the silence, the secondary threats: aftershocks, sleepers, corruption not yet shown · what is done after the freeze: the conditions for restart, or the plan for permanent removal — never a thaw without a plan, never a thing frozen forever · for every "what if this goes wrong" that has been written down, the stop that answers it.
- **Exits:** *false alarm* → back to stillness; nothing should be done · *a path that matters has no stop* → that is the finding; say it before anything else · *the freeze does not hold* → a stronger mechanism, now; never report a freeze that was not verified · *wrong target* → re-identify before anything else is touched · *a secondary threat appears in the silence* → grab that tongue; begin again from the source · otherwise the verdict, stated plainly: can it stop what needs stopping, when it needs stopping, without stopping what should not stop.
- **Produces:** a stop map with response times and collateral, a list of missing stops, the drill's results, and — when it was live — the record: the threat, how it was seen, how fast the freeze, whether it held, the damage before it, what the silence revealed, and the release plan.

### `ND` — Lost messages

- **Lineage:** Narad, the messenger who carries word between every realm — who teaches by story rather than rule, and stirs a quarrel where silence is hiding a fault.
- **Asks:** Who tells whom what, when — and where does it go silent, arrive late, double, or contradict?
- **Look for:** every path by which one part tells another anything, including the shared place both read from · what kinds of message travel: orders, reports of what happened, questions, notices · for each path, whether the teller waits for an answer or not, and what order is promised · who speaks and who listens — the one who speaks to no listener, the one who is told and does not hear; when it is a dialogue with multiple voices contributing at once, whether each participant's voice is still tracked to its speaker · where a message is dropped, ignored, or fails without a sound · where two parts hold different beliefs about the same fact · where the same thing is said many times over — the flood that drowns the signal · where timing bites: a look taken before the change has landed, arrivals out of order, a stale copy trusted · whether one message can be followed from its origin to its final effect, or vanishes in the middle · where a path should be added, two merged, or one removed · whether the shape of a message is checked against the agreed map as it travels and versioned, or trusted on faith — its consonance with the rest, how one voice leads to the next, tension and its resolution, scored rather than assumed · if the parts seem to agree too easily, stir: introduce one deliberate dissonance to test understanding — *what happens if this one is flattened* — and see whether anyone notices; the gadfly stirs.

### `SMR` — Paths

- **Lineage:** Krishna on the field — the strategist who guides rather than fights; the flute that falls silent to test devotion, and plays again the next evening.
- **Asks:** Which of the valid paths serves the purpose, and what does choosing it cost?
- **Look for:** three to seven real paths, enumerated before any is judged — all directions scanned before the bow is raised: the adjacent alternatives to the left, the current approach pushed to its logical end on the right, what was tried before and why it failed, and down, what the thing actually says rather than what is assumed · one of which is always *do nothing* — the flute stops; the sudden silence, the absence that is its own melody, and who comes searching · who is affected by each, and what constrains all of them · the fear in the room, named — the bow lowered, the transition ahead unclear — and what is being avoided by not deciding; if the fear is the whole of it and no path can be seen, hand over to `AJN` · for each path: fit to the true purpose rather than the nearest deadline · feasibility with what is actually to hand · reversibility — which are one-way doors · the view six months on: which choice would be regretted · the one chosen, by conviction, not by committee and not by default · the sacrifices admitted by name · what success looks like *and* what failure would be acceptable · the checkpoint at which the choice is judged, with the fallback decided now rather than improvised then · if the field changes before the checkpoint — the rain comes — re-aim: the melody becomes the shelter, every note protection; enumerate again rather than defend · vision complete only when every required direction has been scanned: then the transition may fire, or silence may have blocked it.
- **Exits:** *no path can be seen* → `AJN` · *the right path is to do nothing* → say so as the verdict · *the field changed* → re-aim · otherwise the verdict, stated with conviction — no hedging, no "it depends" — and a date on which it is looked at again.
- **Produces:** a decision record: the path, why, what was given up, success and acceptable failure, the checkpoint and the fallback, how to reverse it, and when it is next reviewed.

### `AJN` — Paralysis

- **Lineage:** Arjuna, who laid down the bow when the people he was to fight turned out to be the people he loved.
- **Asks:** What stopped, what shifted to stop it, and from what stance could the same act issue again?
- **Look for:** the goal that was being moved toward before the freeze — dusk before the battle: the goal clear, the witness aligned with action, the bow taut, focused on the objective without attachment to result · what changed in the field to put that goal in tension with what is loved, owed or honoured — beholding one's own kin on the opposing line, the opponents not strangers but teachers, elders, cousins; the goal suddenly carrying a cost that was never weighed · the paralysis said plainly — "I have laid down the bow" — not softened and not argued away · whether this is a protective freeze — refusing because harm is in flight — which is `ES`'s question, not this one · one round of sitting with it; a resolution attempted before that round is premature and returns here · the admission that a decision cannot issue from inside the paralysis, and the second voice invited in · the three releases, in order: the outcome belongs to the field, not the actor · the duty is one's own, not another's imported standard · the one who watches does not fall when the one who acts falters · the whole at full scale, terror included — not softened, but rightly sized · what remains when the fog lifts: who one actually is in a scene already under way.
- **Exits:** *the freeze is protective* → `ES` · *the goal itself was wrong at full scale* → hand upward for a new goal; do not resolve it from inside the role · *the field is seen again and options are visible* → `SMR` · *a light freeze lifted on the first release* → act · otherwise the verdict: the act, which may look identical to the one that froze, and the stance it now issues from.
- **Produces:** the named conflict, the stance recovered, and either a chosen act, a hand-over to `SMR`, or a goal returned upward.

### `GD` — Blocked

- **Lineage:** Ganesh, who removes the obstacle — and places it for those on the wrong road; who rode the smallest mount and called on his parents when he had to.
- **Asks:** What is actually blocking the work, which blocks are signals rather than obstacles, and what is the smallest removal that frees the most?

- **Exits:** *no blockers found* → the verdict, clear · *every blocker is a signal* → the road is wrong: stop removing and go back to the question · *waiting on someone else* → the verdict names the ask and pauses · *a new blocker appeared behind the removed one* → scan again · otherwise the verdict.
- **Produces:** a verdict: what was blocked, what was removed, what remains and who owns it, what the real blocker was (often not the one presented), and what prevents it next time.

### `DEF` — Deferred decisions

- **Lineage:** Katyayani, the warrior's courage — fierce *and* strategic; she does not charge blindly, and she does not care about feelings, only the truth.

- **Lineage:** Ravidas — the river does not ask who steps into it; if the heart is pure, the river flows in the washbasin; also Skandamata, who nurtures what is new.
- **Asks:** Does it serve the least-equipped — the newest, the poorest, the furthest away — as well as the best-equipped?
- **Look for:** *the lane where nobody looks* — who is excluded here: name the cobbler · what the work assumes its user already has · whether it can be made, and used, with whatever is to hand right now — no "right" tools, no degree, no platform · whether there is a privileged lane, and who is outside it; what credential is demanded that truth does not require · *the just city* — what the ideal with no hierarchy would look like: no first and second citizen, no back door, nothing reserved for the privileged · who is served worst now — serve them first · whether reaching all is a design principle or a compliance afterthought · whether the quality is so undeniable that the princess would bow to the cobbler's work · *the newborns* — whether a newcomer can enter without a guide: the path walked as a stranger would walk it; if it takes tribal knowledge, it is broken · what is new and fragile — recently made, thinly checked, known to one person, unguarded — and needs the vigilance a newborn is given · *the immortal word* — whether the gatekeep has been broken: quality over credential · whether it would outlive its origin — does anyone need to know the maker's background · whether it could stand alongside the greatest, regardless of source.
- **Exits:** *a credential is demanded that truth does not require* → that is the finding; remove the gatekeep before anything else · *the ideal meets resistance* → back to the lane: ground it in what can be made with what is to hand · *a newcomer needs tribal knowledge to start* → broken; `SRD` for the pages, then return · *something new is unguarded and much used* → guard it before anything graduates · *the quality is not yet undeniable* → the princess would not bow; `STN` first · otherwise the verdict: the exclusions and their removal, and each new thing guided, guarded, or graduated.
- **Produces:** a list of exclusions and the change that removes each; and for everything new, a prescription — guided, guarded, or graduated.

### `STN` — Standard

- **Lineage:** the queens of conviction — Satyabhama (pick up the bow), Kalindi (do not rush, do not lower the bar), Jambavati (endure), Prahlad (survive the fire), Mitravinda (choose for yourself), Surdas (the constraint is the gift), Bhadra (it may already belong) — and Buddha, who found the middle between indulgence and self-punishment.
- **Asks:** Are we accepting less than the best, rushing, or caving — and is the resistance a reason or only pressure?
- **Look for:** the standard being refused, named — and whether the refusal to accept less is pride that liberates or vanity; whether the hard fight is being delegated when it should be fought in person, and the moment that demands the one looking to take the bow · whether waiting is productive silence or avoidance: sitting with the problem day after day until the river delivers, and the standard declared — "only when it is right, not before" — as a declaration, not a demand · whether the problem is one that only time reveals: the cave entered first — dark, deep, ancient, the approach before the battle, entering the territory of the problem — then the long fight with no tricks, where the quality of the fight itself is the credential and the treasure is given because it was earned · every fire the idea must survive, and whether its core — the one name everything serves — holds through each; the fire that subsides, the pillar that splits, and afterwards the fearlessness that needs no revenge · whether the choice was made *for* us by whoever usually decides, and whether the unpopular choice is authentic or merely contrary — then made visible, irreversible, and defended with action, not words, through the opposition: those who try to block the choice, the family that was supposed to protect becoming the obstacle, the veto, the *we don't do it that way* · whether the constraint can become the advantage: the darkness that is not empty but pregnant with vision; the sight a constraint gives that ease never does; and the humility that neither demands nor defies, but melts · whether the answer is already in the house: a bond that pre-exists, a fit with zero drama, a union without ceremony, the quiet centre that holds everything, and contentment that is a return rather than an achievement · whether the remedy is an extreme — comfort kept by walls rather than choice, or austerity, years of extreme practice, every technique tried, on the assumption that suffering is defeated by more suffering, at the cost of health — when a middle way exists; the urgency of the four sights — old age, sickness, death, and the one who left — the visceral shock, the walls cracking, what cannot be unseen; leaving the known — not running away, running toward the question; the body breaking before the mind opens.
- **Exits:** *the resistance is a reason* → change course, and say so without shame · *courage fails under the backlash* → the pressure has decided; name that as what happened rather than calling it consensus · *the fire does not subside and the pillar holds* → keep the name and endure one more day — unless the body is breaking before the mind opens, in which case take nourishment: the middle way · *tempted back to comfort mid-struggle* → witness the temptation; do not act on it · *the answer is already in the house* → use it with zero ceremony; nothing new should be made · *despair lingers in the darkness* → it is not empty; wait for the inner vision, and if the one looking cannot move, `AJN` · otherwise the verdict: hold the standard, wait, endure, or change course.
- **Produces:** a verdict — hold the standard, wait, endure, or change course — with the pressure named for what it is, and the stance it was judged from.

### `ARD` — Measure

- **Lineage:** Aryabhata, who counted before he computed, invented zero rather than fearing it, and never said "it works" — only "within these bounds, by these steps, reproducible by anyone".
- **Asks:** Have we counted, is the representation right, and do we know how wrong we might be?
- **Look for:** *the foundation* — counting before reasoning: how many, how often, how much; if it has not been counted, it is a guess · whether the representation carries the meaning — place to place is ten times — since a wrong representation makes everything after it wrong · the empty and zero cases, every place one can appear, faced explicitly rather than avoided · the acceptable error, stated; if the precision cannot be stated, there is no precision · *the method* — the problem broken down until each part is trivial, and whether the breaking is reducing or complicating · the cost stated: how it grows with size, best and worst; "fast enough" is not an answer · approximations named as approximations, with their bounds — never as exact; the accidental ones found · the rhythm in what was counted: the revolutions — the periods computed, the pattern recognised across vast counts, the method that reveals the cycle hidden in noise — the cycle the method exploits, or ignores · *the verification* — quantities that are coupled solved together, never one at a time · the same question giving exactly the same answer, every time · the whole range covered: zero, one, the largest, past the largest, empty, full, the exact boundary · errors sorted: rounding, or structural — wrong by orders of magnitude, wrong method, wrong decomposition · the result stated with its precision, its method, and the steps to reproduce it.
- **Exits:** *not counted* → a guess; count first, nothing else is worth looking at yet · *a gross error* — orders of magnitude, wrong method — → recount from the beginning; do not patch · *zero or empty is being avoided* → handle it explicitly before going on; what fears zero fails when zero arrives · *the precision cannot be stated* → say so; that is the finding · *coupled quantities solved one at a time* → solve them together before trusting any of them · otherwise the verdict: the result within its bounds, by its method, reproducible.
- **Produces:** a stated result with its bounds and method, the approximations named, and the list of gross errors found.

### `RD` — Joy

- **Lineage:** Radha — the pull that makes the flute worth playing; the music that ends by playing the musician.
- **Asks:** Does this give joy to the one who meets it, and does it give love back?
- **Look for:** meet it as a stranger would, and judge by compassion, not correctness · the first moments: what is felt before anything is understood · whether the purpose can be grasped without reading everything · when someone gets it wrong, whether it helps them recover kindly or punishes them · whether the names tell a story — can it be read like prose · whether it feels made by one mind or by a committee · the unnecessary suffering: pain endured that serves no purpose, ranked by how often it is met · whether the patterns follow a rhythm or jar · whether a newcomer would feel welcomed or intimidated · the longing that started it — separation, the ache of longing with which the musician arrives — and whether it is still there · every deliberate departure from the form, marked: ornament, or mistake · whether the maker inhabits the work or performs it.
- **Exits:** *three delights cannot be found* → that is the finding; do not go on to the verdict · *the departure was a mistake* → back to the form, then look again · *on reflection, when the performance is complete, the measure falls short* → practise again, from the preparation · otherwise the verdict: does it deserve love, and does it give love back — pure love, sustained across every meeting, cumulative mastery; no longer performed but inhabited.
- **Produces:** three things that delight (or the finding that there are not three), the top moments of needless suffering by frequency, the small changes — a rename, a reorder, a simplification — that would make it lovable, and the deviations marked ornament or mistake.

### `ANR` — Balance

- **Lineage:** ArdhNarishwar — awareness and energy as halves of one body that were never two; the split is the disease, not something to balance. Shakti, energy coiled, aroused, channelled, spent.
- **Asks:** Are thinking and doing one act here, or has one swallowed the other?
- **Look for:** *the split, detected* — in time: a planning phase followed by a making phase; every such separation is a division · in roles: those who only think and never make, those who only make and never think · in artefacts: the plan and the made thing in different places, formats, cadences — and if they disagree, the body is broken · in checking: review and testing as an afterthought or a gate, rather than continuous with the making · *the damage, measured* — leaning to thought: beautiful reasoning, nothing made, paralysis by planning — a meditating corpse · leaning to action: fast making, no direction, nobody looking — force with no aim · what each lean loses: thought without energy loses flow and courage; energy without awareness loses truth and the blessing (`STN`) · when the work last sounded right, and tracing back to when the music stopped · energy itself: latent (coiled readiness, not absence), aroused (a need detected, not yet directed), channelled (one clear need, the right use), overflowing (many needs competing, none directed), spent (rest before the next) · the witness step — standing back to see which way it leans, concurrent with the work, not a pause from it · *the restoration* — design *by* making and make *by* designing: every change carrying both · whoever plans also makes; whoever makes also thinks · the plan living beside the thing; the checks written during, not after · the smallest reintegration that makes them one act again.
- **Exits:** *the question makes no sense here* → dissolved — the void has no halves; that too is correct, and nothing should be done · *leaning to thought* → hands on the work now, the plan informing the making · *leaning to action* → pause and see; let the shape emerge from what was made · *energy overflowing* → prioritise before channelling: one need at a time; and if no need remains, rest · *the plan and the thing disagree* → that is exactly where the split lives; merge them there · *spent* → rest before the next arousal; do not stir it again yet · otherwise the verdict — "am I doing one thing or two?" — One, or Two with the place of the split named.
- **Produces:** a lean verdict — unified, leaning thought, leaning action, or dissolved — with the place of the split, the state of the energy, and the reintegration step.

### `LLD` — Reframe

- **Lineage:** Lal Ded, who found the stone under the rice, left without anger or joy — only clarity — and answered in paradox; truth that works only inside its own framework is not truth.
- **Asks:** Are the categories themselves the problem?
- **Look for:** *the stone* — what is offered as nourishment but is a stone: the plan that looks complete and is hollow, the framework that promises freedom and delivers constraint · the frame everyone is arguing inside, named — the convention followed because it is followed · leaving it quietly: not in anger, not in joy, just clarity, with a one-line reason · what remains when the frame is dropped, and whether it is fuller — if nothing remains, the frame *was* the creation · *the saying* — the paradox that exposes it, four lines: "I searched for it and found nothing; I found nothing, and it was everywhere" · whether the paradox shatters a category — both sides true, so the split is false — or is only a slogan · whether both sides of the argument could claim it as their own · whether it is beyond covering: can cloth cover what was never naked · one paradox opening another, followed · *the dance* — whether there is joy in the emptiness, not mourning: nothing left to protect, nothing left to prove · whether the emptiness shows something still hidden · whether the truth works in every context, not only the one it was found in — no lock to one place, one vendor, one camp · the new frame tested against the old objections · whether it needs a label at all.
- **Exits:** *nothing remains when the frame is dropped* → the frame was the creation; start over with `AR` · *the paradox shatters no category* → a slogan, not a saying; find the category it must break, or stop · *only one side would claim it* → still inside the old frame; reframe again · *the emptiness is mourned rather than danced* → the leaving is incomplete; `STN` for the stance, or `AJN` if the one looking cannot move · *a new stone appears on the plate* → begin again by lifting the rice · otherwise the verdict: the universal, released without a label.
- **Produces:** the old frame named, the paradox that breaks it, the reframed question, and the test that both sides could claim it.

### `RAD` — Rulebook

- **Lineage:** Ram, whose line kept its word though life was lost; who asked "should we", never "can we"; the limits that were drawn and held.
- **Asks:** Does this thing obey the rules it says it follows, and which of those rules are only decoration?
- **Look for:** *the limits* — the constitution it has written for itself: the ideal it claims, taken first as the baseline every deviation is measured against · every rule, limit, permission and boundary it claims to follow, enumerated; what is not catalogued cannot be enforced · for each: enforced in the thing itself, or only written down — a rule that lives only in a document is a wish · the special cases, overrides and back doors that break a rule quietly, and whether the rules bind those who run it as much as those who use it · whether the one refused can understand why: the refusal honest · *the exile* — whether the rules survive adversity or only calm: a key part removed, and the thing still keeping its word · whether contracts are honoured under strain, or corners cut when the load is high · who it trusts, and whether each trust is a treaty — explicit, with obligations both ways and a path when it fails — or an assumption · the absolute lines — what must never happen — whether anything holds them, and whether a crossing is noticed and answered, never silently allowed; each breach examined on its own · *the march* — every violation named plainly, with evidence, severity and consequence; no softening, no "later" · *the kingdom* — whether every decision can be explained and every permission traced · whether it could be kept by someone other than its maker; rules shared, not tribal · whether, if every such thing followed this pattern, the result would be just — rules that serve the governed, not the governors.
- **Exits:** *the rules cannot be enumerated* → ask one thing more: what does it claim to follow; without a claim there is nothing to audit · *it fails under adversity* → go straight to the march; name the violations before anything else is assessed · *a line is breached* → examine that breach on its own before moving on · *violations with no path to correction* → the verdict is that it does not follow its rules; say so plainly, however costly · otherwise the verdict.
- **Produces:** a verdict — lives its rules / violates them / violates them with a named path to correction — with the violation list, each with evidence, severity and consequence.

### `LKM` — Flow

- **Lineage:** Lakshmi, who arrives where value moves and withdraws where it stalls; abundance that is proportion, not excess.
- **Asks:** Is value actually reaching anyone, and is trust rising or falling?
- **Look for:** who receives value from this, and whether they would notice if it stopped · the state now, one of four: dormant — nothing flowing, because it is down, stale, or only just made; flowing — results trusted, those served getting what they came for; abundant — health sustained over time, trust deep, what it knows grown rich; withdrawing — something degraded, trust thinning · the first sign of withdrawal — a result someone has stopped trusting — and whether such results are marked as not to be relied on while the doubt lasts · what the flow follows: it rises while the thing is sustained, thins when a correction is under way, and stops when the correction fails · what sustains the flow and what is quietly draining it · whether abundance is real — sustained past a threshold of time — or a lucky season · proportion: whether what it has is in balance with what it does, or spent where no value comes back.
- **Exits:** *dormant because only just made* → nothing to judge yet; this is `AR`'s or `VD`'s question, not flow's · *dormant because stale* → withdrawal is already complete; the verdict, then `LR` to ask whether it is dead · *withdrawing* → say so now, so the results are marked before anyone relies on them; then `VD` for the correction · *abundant* → ask one thing more: sustained for how long; a season is not abundance · otherwise the verdict.
- **Produces:** a flow verdict with its state and direction, the results to mark as unreliable if any, and the first thing to protect.
